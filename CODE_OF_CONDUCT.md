# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project respectful and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while addressing technical evidence directly.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's confidential information.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about scope, safety, and project boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, threats, deliberate disruption, and publication of private information are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
